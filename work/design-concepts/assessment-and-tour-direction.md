# Assessment and role-tour direction

Founder decisions, 2026-09-10:

- Keep the workplace-based 3D Discovery role-tour direction. Preserve the liked Discovery layout.
- Role assessment should retain real work scenarios and mix response interactions: dispatch routing/message, priority ordering/reason, structured handover, spoken customer update.
- A scenario determines a suitable response format; these are not four alternative assessment products.
- The connected sample shift demonstrates all four. The production assessment remains adaptive and must not force every scenario through every format.
- Typed alternatives in this unscored preview are explicitly distinct from spoken evidence.
- No live assessment logic, assistance policy, scoring or backend contracts have been changed. Current answer schemas need deliberate extension before production use.
