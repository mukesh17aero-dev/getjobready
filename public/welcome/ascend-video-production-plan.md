## Narration draft

Your degree builds a foundation. In an AI-shaped job market, turning that knowledge into real work takes more. Ascend helps you discover roles that fit, understand your skill gaps, and practise realistic workplace tasks. Its AI gives specific feedback, so every attempt helps you improve. Prepare for interviews. Build a professional identity backed by what you can do. Colleges can see where students need support. For employers, more applications do not always mean clearer choices. Ascend brings work artifacts, skill evidence and interview readiness into view. Its proposed Productivity Index connects role-specific readiness with the evidence behind it, so employers can judge what has been demonstrated and what still needs development. The aim: better hiring decisions, less time and cost to productivity. Ascend. A talent fulfilment network, bridging the productivity gap.

## Production constraints

Film shots use new camera angles and actions. The welcome footage appears in the film only briefly inside a real welcome-page recording on a device. No recycled full-screen clips. Actual approved app screens replace generated UI. Employer and college capabilities labelled planned when unreleased. No invented numeric scores, social proof, measured savings or job guarantees. Narration and edit timing will be measured before final export.
