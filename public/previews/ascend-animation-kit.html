Read the narration

For students, freshers and those starting their careers, a degree is a foundation. In an AI-shaped world, workplace readiness takes more. Ascend helps you discover suitable roles, identify skill gaps and practise realistic industry tasks. Its AI gives constructive feedback to improve each attempt. Prepare for interviews and build a professional identity backed by your work. Ascend helps you become job-ready and stand out in a crowded field of job seekers. Colleges can focus their support. For employers facing thousands of applications, Ascend brings work artifacts, skill evidence and interview readiness into view. Its proposed Productivity Index connects role-specific readiness with supporting evidence, showing demonstrated capabilities and gaps still to develop. The aim: clearer hiring decisions, with less time and cost to productivity. Ascend. A talent fulfilment network, bridging the productivity gap.
