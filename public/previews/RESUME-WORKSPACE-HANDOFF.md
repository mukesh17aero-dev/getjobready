An active subscriber sees five builder steps: Start, Details, Draft, Feedback, Export. Plans belongs in Account → Subscription & plan. That panel shows verified status, allowances, access period and comparison/upgrade options. The preview's account and payment examples are local fixtures, not billing state.

Production paid AI actions require backend entitlements and allowance checks; manual editing, viewing and permitted downloads should not consume AI drafts. Do not upsell while entitlement verification is pending. Existing users should return to their exact record after authentication/payment. The proposed upgrade flow credits the current pass and retains its expiry; actual billing policy needs backend support.

## Saved resumes and template choice

My resumes opens a searchable drawer rather than occupying the builder's main canvas. The requested record is the default; two other rows are clearly illustrative versions. Production must use actual record IDs, titles, dates and status, and preserve unsaved edits on switching.

Choose a look opens a layout picker with a large rendering of the user's current content, plus thumbnails from the same renderer. Selection is staged; Cancel keeps the current layout and Use layout commits. Starter displays only three included layouts. Plus/Pro display six. Do not expose premium thumbnail images or template names to a lower plan as individual locked cards. A quiet comparison link is sufficient. Existing premium artifacts must remain viewable under the applicable retention policy; restrict new layout selections separately from saved-artifact access.

Reuse the real ResumeSheet/A4Frame renderer for chooser, editor and export. The HTML renderer is a visual fixture and does not add a second production rendering system. Verify multi-page pagination, long names and overflow in the production renderer.
