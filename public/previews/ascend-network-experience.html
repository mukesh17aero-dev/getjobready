WHAT IS ASCEND?

An AI-native
talent fulfillment network.

Bridging the gap between getting hired and becoming productive.Ascend connects students, colleges and employers around one goal: readiness for the actual work. Role discovery, personalised preparation, hands-on practice and evidence of capability form a journey designed to close the productivity gap before day one.
